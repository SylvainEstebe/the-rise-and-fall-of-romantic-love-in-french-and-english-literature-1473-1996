**Figure 1. Romantic affiliation in French and English literature (ING_38).** Panels show French books (a), English books (b), French plays (c), and English plays (d). Grey points are individual ratings jittered for visibility. Blue points show means in calendar-aligned 25-year bins, positioned at the mean publication year, with error bars of ±1.96 standard errors of the mean. Bins are displayed only when they contain at least 10 observations and the interval is no wider than 2 score points. The orange curve shows the posterior mean expected score from a cumulative-logit model with a cubic B-spline in publication year and an author random intercept. One spline per corpus is selected by highest PSIS-LOO ELPD among df = 4, 5, and 6 (French books, df = 6; English books, df = 4; French plays, df = 6; English plays, df = 6). Flagged LOO observations are corrected by exact refitting. Shading shows the pointwise 95% equal-tailed credible interval conditional on the displayed specification. Predictions use four chains × 4,000 retained draws, fix author deviations to zero, and are restricted to the observed publication-year range. Expected scores are Σ k·P(score=k). Alternative specifications and their comparisons are reported in Supplementary Figure S7 and Table S10. Timing estimates in the text summarize dates calculated within each posterior draw and need not coincide with the maximum of the plotted posterior mean curve.
