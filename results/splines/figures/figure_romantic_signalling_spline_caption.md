**Romantic signalling in French and English literature (TH_03).** Panels show French books (a), English books (b), French plays (c), and English plays (d). Grey points are individual ratings jittered for visibility. Blue points show means in calendar-aligned 25-year bins, positioned at the mean publication year, with error bars of ±1.96 standard errors of the mean. Bins are displayed only when they contain at least 10 observations and the interval is no wider than 2 score points. The orange curve shows the posterior mean expected score from a cumulative-logit model with a cubic B-spline in publication year and an author random intercept. One spline per corpus is selected by highest PSIS-LOO ELPD among df = 4, 5, and 6 (French books, df = 4; English books, df = 4; French plays, df = 5; English plays, df = 5). Flagged LOO observations are corrected by exact refitting. Shading shows the pointwise 95% equal-tailed credible interval conditional on the displayed specification. Predictions use four chains × 4,000 retained draws, fix author deviations to zero, and are restricted to the observed publication-year range. Expected scores are Σ k·P(score=k).
