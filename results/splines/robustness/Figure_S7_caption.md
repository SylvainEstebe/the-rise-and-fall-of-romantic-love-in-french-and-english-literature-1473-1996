**Supplementary Figure S7. Sensitivity of long-term courtship-importance trends to spline flexibility.** Panels show French books (a), English books (b), French theatre (c), English theatre (d), and French Babel (e). Blue points show means within calendar-aligned 20-year bins (20 × floor(year/20)), plotted at the mean publication year; error bars are ±1.96 standard errors, and only bins with at least 10 observations are shown. Solid orange, dotted green, and dash-dot purple curves show posterior mean expected scores from cubic B-splines with df 4, 5, and 6, respectively; the dashed grey curve shows the quadratic model. The light band is the pointwise 95% equal-tailed credible interval for the highest-ELPD spline among the three displayed models in each panel (French books, df 6; English books, df 4; French theatre, df 6; English theatre, df 6; French Babel, df 6). ELPD comparisons use PSIS-LOO with exact refits for flagged observations. Each cumulative-logit model includes an author random intercept and uses four chains of 4,000 retained draws. Curves show Σ k·P(score=k), with author deviations set to zero, and are restricted to each corpus’s observed publication-year range. Shading does not include model-selection uncertainty.
