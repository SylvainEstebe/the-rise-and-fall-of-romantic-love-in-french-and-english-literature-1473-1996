both sound asleep already
and farewell both for one short moment
trumpet sound victory within
those be the foe we little band be lost
for want of these defender i must hasten
lest i be forced to live and lead in triumph
defraud of i fate i have earned it well
and finished all i task this be i place
just at i master foot guard he yee god
and save he sacred corps from publick shame
he fall on he sword and lie at the foot of cleom die
it be what i heart forebode there he lie
extended by the man whom best he loved
a good friend than son
what be he or thou or ptolomy or aegypt
or all the world to cleomenes lost
then i suspected right if i revenge
can ease i sorrow this the king shall know
that thou mayst reap the due reward of treason
and violate love
thou bad old dotard
i wish to die but if i mind should change
so well i know i power that thou art lost
the king arrival shall decide we fate
mean time to show how much i honour virtue
take up that hero body bear it high
like the procession of a deity
let he arm figure on he tomb be set
and we like slave lie grovling at he foot
whose glory grow till he late breath
excel all other and he own in death
ex omne
