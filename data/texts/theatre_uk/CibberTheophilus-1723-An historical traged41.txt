away i say i charge you bear she hence
so come to you and yours as to this prince
to london in great haste and as i guess
to make a bloody supper in the tower
he be sudden if a thing come in he head
now march we hence discharge the common sort
with pay and thank and let we away to london
and see how well we gentle queen do fare
by this i hope she hath a son for i
now here a period of tumultuous broil
once more we fit on england royal throne
repurchasd with the blood of enemy
what valiant foemen like to autumn corn
have we mow down in top of all she pride
three duke of somerset threefold renowned
for hardy and undoubted champion
two cliffords as the father and the son
and two northumberlands two brave man
never spur they courser at the trumpet sound
with they the two brave bear warwick and mountague
that in they chain fettered the kingly lion
and make the forest tremble when they roar
thus have we sweep suspicion from we seat
and make we footstool of security
what will you grace have do with margaret
away with she and waft she hence to france
and then what rest but that we spend the time
with stately triumph mirthful comick shew
such as befit the pleasure of the court
so to divert we subject busy mind
from do farther mischief against the state
sound drum and trumpet farewel all annoy
for here i hope begin we last joy
and may this land learn from we house jar
ever to dread the event of civil war
