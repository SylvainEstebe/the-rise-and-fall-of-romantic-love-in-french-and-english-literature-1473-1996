romeo there dead be husband to that juliet
and she there dead that romeo faithful wife
i married they and they steal marriage day
be tybalt doomsday whose untimely death
banished the newmade bridegroom from this city
for whom and not for tybalt juliet pine
you to remove that siege of grief from she
betroth and would have marry she perforce
to county paris then come she to i
and with wild look bid i devise some mean
to rid she from this second marriage
or in i cell there would she kill herself
then give i she so tutore by i art
a sleep potion which so take effect
as i intended for it work on she
the form of death meantime i writ to romeo
that he should hither come as this dire night
to help to take she from she borrow grave
be the time the potion force should cease
but he which bear i letter friar john
be stay by accident and yesternight
return i letter back then all alone
at the prefixed hour of she wake
come i to take she from she kindred vault
meaning to keep she closely at i cell
till i conveniently could send to romeo
but when i come some minute ere the time
of she awaken here untimely lie
the noble paris and true romeo dead
she wake and i entreat she come forth
and bear this work of heaven with patience
but then a noise do scare i from the tomb
and she too desperate would not go with i
but as it seem do violence on herself
all this i know and to the marriage
she nurse be privy and if aught in this
miscarry by i fault let i old life
be sacrifice some hour before he time
unto the rigor of severe law
we still have known thou for a holy man
where be romeo man what can he say to this
i bring i master news of juliet death
and then in post he come from mantua
to this same place to this same monument
this letter he early bid i give he father
and threatened i with death going in the vault
if i departed not and left he there
give i the letter i will look on it
where be the county page that raised the
watch
sirrah what make you master in this place
he come with flower to strew he lady grave
and bid i stand aloof and so i do
anon come one with light to ope the tomb
and by and by i master draw on he
and then i run away to call the watch
this letter do make good the friar word
they course of love the tiding of she death
and here he write that he do buy a poison
of a poor apothecary and therewithal
come to this vault to die and lie with juliet
where be these enemy capulet montague
see what a scourge be laid upon you hate
that heaven find means to kill you joy with love
and i for wink at you discord too
have lost a brace of kinsman all be punished
o brother montague give i thou hand
this be i daughter jointure for no more
can i demand
but i can give thou more
for i will ray she statue in pure gold
that while verona by that name be known
there shall no figure at such rate be set
as that of true and faithful juliet
as rich shall romeo by he lady lie
poor sacrifice of we enmity
a glooming peace this morning with it bring
the sun for sorrow will not show he head
go hence to have more talk of these sad thing
some shall be pardon and some punished
for never be a story of more woe
than this of juliet and she romeo
