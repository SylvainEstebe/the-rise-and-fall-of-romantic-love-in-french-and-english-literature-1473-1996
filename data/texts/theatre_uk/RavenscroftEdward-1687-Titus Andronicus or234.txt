that sill you house with all this ruine
it be decree he should expire in flame
around he kindle streight he funeral fire
the matter be prepared now let it blaze
the fire flame about the moor
he shall at once be burnt and rack to death
wherefore should rage be mure and fury dumb
ten thousand worser ill then re i do
would i perform if i might have i will
if one good deed in all i life i do
i now repent it from i very heart
for proof i do le curse you till i dye
vengeance and blewest plague consume you all
in howling end thou life
now convey the emprour to he father tomb
as for that hatefull tygress tamora
no right nor funerall ceremony
i noble father and lavinia
shall be closed in we housaslhold monument
roman and friend assist you all a while
when these sad ceremony be perform
lead i to empire crown i if you please
but nothing this afflict heart can ease
