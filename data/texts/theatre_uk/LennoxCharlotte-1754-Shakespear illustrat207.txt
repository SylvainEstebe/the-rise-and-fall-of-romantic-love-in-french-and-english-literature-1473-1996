king leir once ruled in this land
with princely power and peace
and have all thing with heart content
that might he joy increase
amongst those thing that nature give
three daughter fair have he
so princely seeming beautiful
as fair could not be
so on a time it pleased the king
a question thus to move
which of he daughter to he grace
could shew the dear love
for to i age you bring content
quoth he then let i hear
which of you three in plighted troth
the kind will appear
to whom the eldest thus begin
dear father mind quoth she
before you face to do you good
i blood shall render be
and for you sake i bleed heart
shall here be cut in twain
re that i see you reverend age
the small grief sustain
and so will i the second said
dear father for you sake
the bad of all extremity
i will gently undertake
and serve you highness night and day
with diligence and love
that sweet content and quietness
discomfort may remove
in do so you glad i soul
the age king reply
but what sayest thou i young girl
how be thou love allied
i love
which to you grace i owe
shall be the duty of a child
and that be all i will show
and wilt thou shew no more quoth he
than do thou duty bind
i well perceive thou love be small
when as no more i find
henceforth i banish thou i court
thou art no child of mine
nor any part of this i realm
by favour shall be thine
thou eldest sister love be more
than well i can demand
to whom i equally bestow
i kingdom and i land
i pompous state and all i goods
that lovingly i may
with those thou sister be maintained
until i die day
thus flatter speech win renown
by these two sister here
the third have causeless banishment
yet be he love more dear
for poor cordelia patiently
go wandering up and down
unhelped unpitied gentle maid
through many an english town
until at last in famous france
she gentl fortune find
though poor and bare yet she be deem
the fair on the ground
where when the king she virtue hear
and this fair lady see
with full consent of all he court
he make he wife and queen
she father old king lear this while
with he two daughter staid
forgetful of they promise love
full soon the same deny
and living in queen regans court
the eldest of the twain
she take from he he chief means
and most of all he train
for whereas twenty man be will not
to wait with bended knee
she give allowance but to ten
and after scarce to three
nay one she think too much for he
so take she all away
in hope that in she court good king
he would no long stay
be i rewarded thus quoth he
in giving all i have
unto i child and to beg
for what i lately give
ll go unto i gonoril
i second child i know
will be more kind and pitiful
and will relieve i woe
full fast he hies then to she court
where when she hear he moan
return he answer that she grieved
that all he means be go
but no way could relieve he want
yet if that he would stay
within she kitchen he should have
what scullion give away
when he have hear with bitter tear
he make he answer then
in what i do let i be make
example to all man
i will return again quoth he
unto i regans court
she will not use i thus i hope
but in a kind sort
where when he come she give command
to drive he thence away
when he be well within she court
she said he would not stay
then back again to gonorill
the woful king do hie
that in she kitchen he might have
what scullion boy set by
but there of that he be denied
which she have promise late
for once refuse he should not
come after to she gate
thus atwixt he daughter for relief
he wander up and down
be glad to feed on beggar food
that lately wear a crown
and calling to remembrance then
he young daughter word
that said the duty of a child
be all that love afford
but doubting to repair to she
whom he have banished so
grow frantick mad for in he mind
he bear the wound of woe
which make he rend he milkwhite lock
and tress from he head
and all with blood bestain he cheek
with age and honour spread
to hill and wood and watry fount
he make he hourly moan
till hill and wood and sensless thing
do seem so sigh and groan
even thus possess with discontent
he passed re to france
in hope from fair cordelia there
to find some gentl chance
most virtuous dame which when she hear
of this she father grief
as duty bound she quickly send
he comfort and relief
and by a train of noble peer
in brave and gallant sort
she give in charge he should be bring
to aganippus court
whose royal king whose noble mind
so freely give consent
to muster up he knight at arm
to fame and courage bent
and so to england come with speed
to repossess king lear
and drive he daughter from they throne
by he cordelia dear
where she truehearted noble queen
be in the battel slain
yet he good king in he old day
possessed he crown again
but when he hear cordelia death
who die indeed for love
of she dear father in whose because
she do this battel move
he swooning fell upon she breast
from whence he never part
but on she bosom left he life
that be so truly hearted
the lords and noble when they see
the end of these event
the other sister unto death
they doomed by consent
and be dead they crown they left
unto the next of kin
thus have you see the fall of pride
and disobedient sin
