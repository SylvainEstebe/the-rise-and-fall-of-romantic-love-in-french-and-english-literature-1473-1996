counting myself but bad till i be good
i will throw thou body in another room
and triumph henry in thou day of doom
clarence richard of gloucester hastings nurse
carrying infant prince edward and attendant

once more we sit in england royal throne
repurchase with the blood of enemy
what valiant foemen like to autumn corn
have we mow down in top of all they pride
three duke of somerset threefold renowned
for hardy and undoubted champion
two cliffords as the father and the son
and two northumberlands two brave man
never spur they courser at the trumpet sound
with they the two brave bear warwick and
montague
that in they chain fettered the kingly lion
and make the forest tremble when they roar
thus have we sweep suspicion from we seat
and make we footstool of security
come hither bess and let i kiss i boy
young ned for thou thou uncle and myself
have in we armors watch the winter night
go all afoot in summer scalding heat
that thou mightst repossess the crown in peace
and of we labor thou shalt reap the gain

i will blast he harvest if you head be laid
for yet i be not look on in the world
this shoulder be ordained so thick to heave
and heave it shall some weight or break i back
work thou the way and that shalt execute

clarence and gloucester love i lovely queen
and kiss you princely nephew brother both
the duty that i owe unto you majesty
i seal upon the lip of this sweet babe

thank noble clarence worthy brother thank
and that i love the tree from whence thou spring
witness the love kiss i give the fruit
to say the truth so juda kiss he master
and cry all hail whenas he mean all harm

now be i seated as i soul delight
have i country peace and brother love
what will you grace have do with margaret
reignier she father to the king of france
hath pawn the sicils and jerusalem
and hither have they send it for she ransom

away with she and waft she hence to france
and now what rest but that we spend the time
with stately triumph mirthful comic show
such as befit the pleasure of the court
sound drum and trumpet farewell sour annoy
for here i hope begin we last joy
