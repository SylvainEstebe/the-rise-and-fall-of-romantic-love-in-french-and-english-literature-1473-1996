Much more might be said in this case to render Virtue Lovely and desirable in the Eyes of Men; but seeing she has not only been Recommended to them by our Blessed Saviour, the Fountain of Virtue and Goodness, but by the Prophets, Apostles, and Good Men of all Ages; those who refuse to Entertain her upon such Recommendations, will hardly be induc'd to it by any endeavour of Mine. So to conclude this General, I shall only add, That Virtue is a Work of GOD in Man, and not of Man in himself; for as the Rain moisteneth the Ground, to render it Fruitful, and yet the Rain is not the Ground, nor the Ground the Fruit; Even so in the Ground of our Souls he Infuses his Grace, to render us Fruitful in Good Works.
