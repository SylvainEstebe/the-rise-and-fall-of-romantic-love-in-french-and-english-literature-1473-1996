S. GEORGE Faire Englands Patrone of Renowne and fame. Whome Sathan men. nor monsters Could not tame Avaliant Champion both in age and youth. And Last a glorious Martir for the Truth [Page] [Page] S. DENNIS Next vnder God, in france he was the cheife, To make them know the Christian True Beleife, His worthy Constancy did neuer faint. But dide a martir as he liu'd a Saint. [Page] [Page] S. IAMES In Spaine his memory they celebrate Cheife patrone of that high and mighty state, Whose life was vertuous and his Acts victorious And in the end his death was blest and glorious. In Ireland, 'mongst the Rough, Vnruly Kerns[?] He taught, what their saluation Most Concernes. His Constancy and Courage- Neuer, Faild. He alwaies Liu'd Beloud and die'd Bewaild [Page] [Page] S. DAUID In Wales this Blessed man the patrone is Who did instruct Them in the way to Blisse. Whose words and valiant Deed [...] did win Such Loue. That pleased men on earth and god aboue.
