IT is a course very familiar amongst the most notorious malefactors at New-Gate, that about a week before the Sessions when they are certaine they shall be produced upon the Stage to receive a Tryall agreeable to Law; and so each man be censured according to his demerit, Doe summon the whole Crue of Malignants together; and at a publike Councel then held( amongst themselves) nominate and appoint a part for a Recorder and Iustices, some a Clerk of the Peace, and other Officers, which attend at everp Sessions, and another part for a Iury, and the rest prisoners for tryall: which done, thePage 4 Image 003-aRecorder and Iustices take their Seates, the Clerk of the Peace, and other Officers, their Places of Attendance, and then with all the Ceremony ordinary at Sessions, the Iury is impannelled, and the prisoners brought to tryall; which done, the Recorder and Iustices, with the prisoners change Seates, and so by turnes, impeach and condemn one another. Now for that, many of these prisoners are men very knowing in the Rules of Iustice, and the methodick Tryals at Sessions in regard of their long experience; and because they are acquainted with each others guilt, and what can be brought in Evidence against them; Doe hereby give the nearest guesse who shall he freed, who suffer, and by what punishment ▪ In the very same manner it is with this black Guard of Antichrist; they conceive their time of Tryall cannot bee long, and that they are likely each man to be rewarded according to his offence they do here summon themselvs together, and by turns sentence each other, and in all probability it followes, that these Cater-pillars being as notorious malefactors, as either now, or ever were in New-gate, and as knowing men in their wickednesse of each other; & what punishment the Iustice of our English Lawes allow them, can with as much ease distinguish, and censure each others offences as the eldest Theeves in all New-gate. What say you? Is Ignatius Holywater guilty of that he stands indicted of, or not guilty. Iury,Guilty. Clerk P.Take him Gaoler. Cl. P.Ignatius Holy-water, thou hast bin here indicted, and upon a legall triall hast bin found guilty; therefore thy Sentence is this: thou art to depart hence to the place thou came from; and from thence to the place of Execution, and there to hang by the neck till thou die, And so thou mayst say we are mercifull unto thee.
