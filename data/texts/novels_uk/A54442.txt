Chap. 43.Of something worth your Hearing. NOW when the Palatine had thus miscarri'd in a Barbarous and unprincely Project, which he thought could never have fail'd, he whistled all his Sea-horses together, and returning back to the Coast of Albion, he shut them up in a wide Waterstable, or Gulph of the Sea, call'd the Gulph of Dolthuso, and barricado'd 'em up with an invisible Chain of daring boldness to vain-glorious presumption, a mile in circumference, and thirty Leagues in length, which reach'd from one end of the Bay to the other. Now having, as he thought, thus secur'd all his Seahorses from all assaults of the Belgians, he went himself ashore, attended by the greatest number of his Tritons. For you must know, that after the ill success of the Saracens, the Palatine had conceiv'd another design in his head, and by the terrible painsPage 125 Image 068-bwhich he felt in his scull, he was of opinion that his time of Delivery was by. To supply therefore the want of Jupiter's Midwife, ( for there was not one among all his Tritons or Phocas's that knew how to cleave a scull, and close it together again) the Palatine sent for a Son of Esculapius to open his scull with a Trepan: Who had no sooner made a hole on both sides the Coronal Suture about the breadth of an Astopian Crown piece, ( which the Artist was above two hours a-doing, for the Palatines scull was very hard and thick) but out leap'd an Embrio of a Project in the nature of a Bears Cub, without form or shape; which the Palatine therefore undertook to lick into Lineaments. But when he had lickt it, till his Tongue was almost as dry as the out-side of a Westphalie-Ham, he could never lick it to such a perfection, but that it still retain'd a hideous Monstrosity. For as the Annals of those times report, it was to have delv'd under the Sea till he came to the Shore of the lower Belgium, and so to have undermin'd all the Country as far as Ultrajectum one way, to the strong Town of Obtricum another way, in order to the blowing up of all the Confederated Belgium, Towns, Cities, Families, Men, Women, Virgins, Children, Cattel and all. To this purpose he sent for Gog and Magog, and made him his chief Engineer and Overseer of his Works, who brought along with him a Legion of Peruvian Miners accustom'd to delve under the Earth, in search of the great God of the World Aurum. To which, the Palatine having joyn'd his own Phocas's, they had proceeded so far, that in ten days they were got within forty Fathom of the Foundations of the Northern Hemisphere. Page 126 Image 069-a
