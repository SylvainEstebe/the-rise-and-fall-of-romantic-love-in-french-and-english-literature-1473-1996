IN Faguell a fayre countre A great lorde somtyme dyd dwell Which had a lady so fayre and fre That all men good of her dyd tel ¶ Fayre and pleasaunt she was in sight Gentyl and amyable in eche degre Chaste to her lorde bothe day and nyght As is the turtyll vpon the tre ¶ All men her loued bothe yonge and olde For her vertue and gentylnesse Also in that lande was a knight bolde Ryght wyse and ful of doughtinesse ¶ All men spake of his hardynesse Ryche and poore of eche degre So that they called him doutlesse The noble knyght of curtesy ¶ This knight so curtesy was and bolde That the lorde
