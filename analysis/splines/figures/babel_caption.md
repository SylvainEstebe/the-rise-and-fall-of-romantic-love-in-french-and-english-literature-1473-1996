Blue points show means in calendar-aligned 25-year bins at their mean publication year, with ±1.96 SEM error bars; bins require at least 10 ratings and an interval width no greater than 2 points. Orange curves show posterior mean expected scores from cumulative-logit models with cubic B-splines and author random intercepts. Each panel uses the highest LOO ELPD among df = 4, 5, 6, with flagged observations corrected by exact refitting. Pale orange bands show pointwise 95% equal-tailed credible intervals conditional on the displayed model. Predictions use four chains × 4,000 retained draws, set author deviations to zero and remain within each observed year range. Expected scores are Σ k·P(score=k).
